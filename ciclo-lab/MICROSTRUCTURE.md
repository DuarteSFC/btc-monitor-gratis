## MS1 — fixed-calendar expansion and first absorption hypothesis
2026-11-27. MS0 verified six files and 12,771,024 aggregate executions. Minute-level mismatch is not ignored: a one-minute volume error over 1% OR OHLC discrepancy greater than 0.1% excludes that minute from microstructure feature windows. This quality filter compares two historical sources after the minute closes; it is a retrospective eligibility proxy, not guaranteed live availability. No missing five-second bins filled.
- Fixed ten complete weeks: Feb 1–7, May 1–7, Aug 1–7, Nov 1–7 in 2022 and 2023; Apr 1–7 and Jul 1–7 in 2026. No MS0 days included, no 2024–25. Calendar stratification, not outcome selection. Each day SHA-verified, 5s summaries reconciled to certified 1m futures bars. Archive per-day source and output hashes and quality counts. Do not silently drop failed days.
- MS01 hypothesis: a surge of aggressive selling that produces unusually little downward price movement, followed by short buying pressure, may indicate selling exhaustion. At each completed UTC minute boundary t, shock window [t-75s,t-15s), recovery [t-15s,t). Shock volume >=2x trailing median of prior complete 60s rolling volumes (30 minutes, ending before shock), sell fraction >=0.65, shock return between -1x trailing median absolute 60s return and zero. Recovery buy fraction >=0.60 and positive price return. All required 5s bins present; no backward fill, normalization uses only prior observations, quality filter over entire feature history. Zero denominators abstain. No model fit or threshold sweep. Signals only during fixed sample days, with warmup and last four hours of each week excluded. 
- Compare to a matched base event (same shock volume, sell fraction and recovery conditions, without the limited price-response filter). Compute 120-minute hold with 1.5 ATR(14) of completed 1h futures bars stop, entry 1/5/15 minutes after t, both established cost scenarios and realized funding, 0.25% equity risk, one position, no leverage. ATR uses only completed candles (perpetual prices, not spot). One policy, 12 replays across 2022–23 and 2026; base comparison and matched controls on 1m stress only. Controls matched by day and 6h session if possible; exact counts reported. No profit claim based on this sparse calendar subset.

## MS03 — buy-side execution-size asymmetry during a breakout
